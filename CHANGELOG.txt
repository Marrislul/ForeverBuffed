0.0.3

FIXES
- Paladin: Judgement no longer makes the seal reminder pop up. On Forever, Judgement doesn't use up your seal, it stays active.
- Buffs that don't stack now count both ways, also for your own reminders. Track an Intellect elixir and a mage's Arcane Intellect replaces it? The elixir reminder now counts Arcane Intellect as buffed instead of nagging.
  Groups: Intellect (Arcane Intellect / Brilliance, Boosted Intellect, Scrolls of Intellect, Intellect elixirs), Stamina (Fortitude, Boosted Stamina, Scrolls of Stamina), Spirit (Divine Spirit, Boosted Spirit, Scrolls of Spirit), Mark of the Wild (Boosted Stats from the Enchanted Lute).
  Your own reminders get the "Non-stacking buffs count" switch too.
