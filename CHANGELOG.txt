0.0.7

FIXES
- Buffs you cast on yourself in combat count again, e.g. Blessing of Might, Arcane Intellect or Power Word: Fortitude. Because of the surnames on Forever, the addon thought they went to someone else. Only the group versions and self-only buffs like Battle Shout worked.
- Paladins: Blessing of Freedom or Protection on yourself replaces your own blessing. The blessing reminder now counts down until it runs out, so you know when to rebuff, also in combat. Before, it showed your blessing as missing right away out of combat, and in combat not until the end of the fight.
- Cancelling your Blessing of Protection early (right-click or /cancelaura) shows the blessing reminder right away.
