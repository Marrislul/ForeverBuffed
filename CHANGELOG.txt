0.0.4

FIXES
- Paladin: Seal of Command's extra damage no longer restarts the seal timer. After a few hits the seal reminder could stay away in combat even though the seal had run out. Only casts you start yourself count now, procs that share a buff's name are ignored.

NEW
- /fb buffs also shows how long each buff has left.
- /fb debug explains in chat what the addon sees (casts, buff checks). Handy if you want to report a bug.
