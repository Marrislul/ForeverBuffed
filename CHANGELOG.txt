0.0.6

FIXES
- Your own buffs cast in combat no longer take their duration from someone else's. Another warrior's Battle Shout with Booming Voice lasts longer, and after seeing it the addon expected yours to last that long too, so the reminder came too late.
- A fishing pole no longer counts as a weapon: no poison, imbue or weapon buff reminder while you fish.
- Learning a new rank checks the buffs of others right away. A Fortitude rank 4 from someone else stops counting as soon as you learn rank 5, not only once your buffs change.
- While blessings of other paladins cover yours, the warning before one of them runs out shows that blessing, not the one you used last.
