0.0.2

LAYOUT
- New grow directions "Centered, horizontal" and "Centered, vertical": the bar stays centered on its spot, however many reminders are showing.
- Changing the icon size now grows the bar evenly in all directions instead of from a corner.
- Type in the exact position: Position X / Y, measured from the screen center.
- Your current position carries over automatically.

GLOW
- Glow can now be set per reminder: off, when missing, or when missing or running out.
- Missing glows red, running out glows orange. Great for Battle Shout: let it glow before it drops, so you can build up rage in time.

NEW REMINDERS (off by default)
- Camp Benefits: the buff from sitting at a campfire.
- Tracking (any): Find Herbs, Find Minerals, Sense Undead, the hunter tracks... Only one tracking can be on at a time, so any of them counts.

BUFFS THAT DON'T STACK
Some buffs block a class buff. Those now count as buffed:
- Arcane Intellect: Boosted Intellect (camp), Scrolls of Intellect, Intellect elixirs
- Fortitude: Boosted Stamina (camp), Scrolls of Stamina
- Divine Spirit: Boosted Spirit (camp), Scrolls of Spirit
- Mark of the Wild: Boosted Stats (camp, Enchanted Lute)
Rather replace a weaker scroll with your own buff? Turn off "Non-stacking buffs count" for that reminder.

OTHER
- The "Class & Weapons" tab is now called "Class & General".
